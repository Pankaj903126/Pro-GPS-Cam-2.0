# Privacy Policy for Pro GPS Cam

**Effective Date:** October 18, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 18, 2026  

Pro GPS Cam ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application **Pro GPS Cam** ("App") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

### A. Location Data
* **Data Collected:** Precise location (GPS coordinates: latitude and longitude), altitude, accuracy, and compass orientation.
* **Purpose:** The primary feature of Pro GPS Cam is to create georeferenced survey photos and videos. Location data is processed on-device to draw location stamps (city, state, road, GPS coordinates, compass heading) onto your photos and videos.
* **Network Reverse Geocoding:** To display readable addresses on your stamps, your GPS coordinates are sent to third-party reverse-geocoding services:
  * **OpenStreetMap Nominatim** (`nominatim.openstreetmap.org`)
  * **IP-API** (`ipapi.co`) for IP location fallback when GPS is disabled or unavailable.
* **Sharing:** Location data is stamped onto the media files saved directly to your device gallery. We do not transmit or store your location history on any external app server.

### B. Camera and Microphone Data
* **Data Collected:** Camera video feeds, still images, and audio recorded via device microphone.
* **Purpose:** To allow you to take survey photos and record survey videos with embedded GPS watermark overlays.
* **Storage:** Photos and videos are saved locally on your device in the standard media gallery. Audio is only recorded when you take a video recording and have granted microphone permissions. We do not access or transmit your camera feed or audio off your device.

### C. Device Storage and Media Files
* **Data Collected:** Access to device media storage (MediaStore / Photo Picker).
* **Purpose:** To save stamped survey photos and videos to your device's photo gallery and allow you to view, edit, or restamp existing photos.

### D. Advertising and Device Identifiers
* **Data Collected:** Advertising ID, IP address, device model, system performance metrics, and general diagnostic logs collected by Google AdMob.
* **Purpose:** To serve non-personalized or personalized banner and rewarded advertisements within the App to support development.
* **Third-Party SDKs:**
  * **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob):** [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
  * **Google User Messaging Platform (UMP):** Used to obtain and manage user consent preferences in compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and Google Play policies.

## 2. Accounts and Login
Pro GPS Cam does **not** require any account creation, registration, email address, password, or login credentials. All your survey photos, videos, and settings remain under your direct control on your device.

---

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion
* All photos, videos, and stamped metadata are stored locally on your device.
* You can delete your survey media files at any time using your device's built-in file manager or gallery application.
* Uninstalling the App will remove temporary caches and local application preferences.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy
Pro GPS Cam is a professional utility tool intended for general audiences and professional land surveyors. The App is **not** directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take immediate action.

## 5. Security
We prioritize the protection of your data. The App uses secure HTTPS connections for all reverse-geocoding API requests and AdMob communications.

## 6. Your Rights and Privacy Controls

* **Ad Privacy Options:** You can update or revoke your ad consent preferences at any time via the "Privacy Options" setting inside the App's Settings menu.
